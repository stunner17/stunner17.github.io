About

I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at Stony Brook University, advised by Dr. Jun Wang in the Multiplex Biotechnology Laboratory. I develop machine learning methods for highly multiplexed single-cell spatial proteomics generated with Multiplex In Situ Tagging (MIST), with the goal of finding disease-associated biomarkers and cell phenotypes, and predicting them directly from routine histopathology.

Before Stony Brook, I completed an M.S. in Biological Engineering at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln with Dr. Hyun-Seob Song, where I built models that couple genomics, thermodynamics, and multi-species kinetics to study subsurface microbial communities. My undergraduate work used deep CNNs and transfer learning for medical image classification. I also build tools for LLM agents, including MCP servers.
